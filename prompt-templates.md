# Media Generator Prompt Templates

Ready-to-use prompts for content marketing assets. Adapt to your specific topic and brand.

## The ICS Formula

Every prompt should follow: **[Subject] + [Setting] + [Lighting] + [Camera] + [Style]** — keep under 25 words for best results.

Example: "Professional woman presenting data on laptop, modern office, soft natural light, medium shot, clean corporate photography"

## Tips for Better Results

1. **Be specific** — "red 2024 Toyota Camry" beats "car"
2. **Describe lighting** — "soft golden hour" or "dramatic side lighting"
3. **Specify camera** — "medium shot" or "bird's eye view"
4. **Add style** — "flat design" or "cinematic photography"
5. **Keep it short** — Under 25 words for image, under 50 for video
6. **For text in images** — Use all caps, specify font style, keep to 3-5 words max
7. **For Arabic text** — Use the overlay command or Remotion stills, never rely on Gemini
8. **For consistency** — Use reference images from previous generations
